About

I am Wenhao Liu. I completed my undergraduate studies at Shanghai University and am currently pursuing a master's degree there.

My interests are machine learning systems, large language models, AI agents, and the infrastructure that makes them practical. I spend most of my time building, training, and debugging systems that sit somewhere between research and production.

This blog is where I write about deep learning, language models, agent systems, software engineering, and the technical details that I want to understand more clearly. Most posts are notes from projects, experiments, papers, and problems I encounter along the way.

This blog serves as a public notebook for ideas worth keeping and lessons worth sharing.
